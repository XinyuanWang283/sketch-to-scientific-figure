# Candidate-specific ImageGen direction summaries

All five candidates use the same hand-drawn sketch and the same scientific invariants from [`clarification_brief.md`](clarification_brief.md). Each candidate was produced with a separate Codex built-in ImageGen call. No previous candidate was supplied as a reference to another call.

These are concise direction summaries for public review, not verbatim backend request payloads. The repository does not claim that these summaries alone prove call independence or generator identity.

## A — Faithful

Clean and professional redraw that preserves the sketch's left-to-right placement, right-side vertical comparison, and lower equation band. Restrained navy, teal, and muted purple accents; flat vector-like shapes; minimal decoration.

## B — Publication

Compact journal-figure treatment. Mostly grayscale with muted blue and amber semantic accents, thin strokes, strong alignment, and no decorative shadows.

## C — Presentation

Presentation-friendly hierarchy with stronger grouping, dimensional generator blocks, and a clearly visible optimization loop.

## D — Alternative layout

Reorganize only the spatial composition. Emphasize the generator and reconstruction as the central visual story, with a compact data-consistency loop and unmistakable feedback path.

## E — Visual variant

High-end Science Day treatment with restrained dimensionality, generous whitespace, and a blue-violet-teal palette while preserving the same scientific topology.

## Shared exclusions

Do not introduce training data, a ground-truth image, supervised learning, performance claims, titles, paper branding, watermarks, or extra modules. Generated typography and equations remain non-authoritative until editable reconstruction.
